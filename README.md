### 🪪 一张名片，装下一个具体的人

我想做的数字名片，可以放下你的作品、兴趣、正在琢磨的想法，也可以是一段视频、一个小工具，或者还没完成的计划。

> 让别人看见你做的事，也让你遇见能一起做事的人。

### 🌱 从一个想法，到自己的作品

我在尝试用 AI 把这条路变短一点。做小产品、折腾创作工具、拍视频，也把一路试出来的方法放在这里。

有些已经能用，有些还在慢慢长大。我自己的数字名片，也在边做边改。

### A small card. A whole person.

I want everyone to have a digital home of their own—a place for the things they make, the ideas they are exploring, and the interests that make them who they are. A video, a tiny tool, or even an unfinished project belongs there.

> Let your work help people find you—and help you find people to build with.

I'm exploring how AI can shorten the distance between an idea and something you can share. This is where I keep my small products, creative tools, experiments, and the things I learn along the way.

Some work already. Some are still growing. My own digital home is a work in progress, too.

**If you want to make a little corner of the internet your own, I'd love to hear from you.**
